# Forklift Brand Identity

- **Name:** `forklift` (lowercase wordmark, `Forklift` in prose)
- **Primary Tagline:** Heavy lifting for your forks.
- **Secondary Tagline:** Keep forks up to date. Automatically.
- **Personality:** Industrial Braun / Teenage Engineering utility meets modern dev-tool precision. Blunt, mechanical, dependable—never cartoonish construction clipart. Use hazard stripes only as a restrained 4px accent bar.

- Visual identity sheet: [`brand/forklift-brand.html`](./brand/forklift-brand.html)

## Color Palette

### Neutrals (Matte Graphite)
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Chassis | `#101214` | Primary dark surface |
| Steel | `#181B1F` | Cards & panels |
| Bolt | `#262B31` | Borders & dividers |
| Primer | `#8B949E` | Secondary metadata |
| Stencil | `#F6F4EE` | Light paper surface |

### Signals
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Safety Yellow | `#F5B700` | Brand mark & primary CTAs |
| Caution | `#D97706` | Hook conflicts / needs review |
| Signal Green | `#22C55E` | Clean sync & passing checks |
| Redline | `#EF4444` | Brand leaks / failures |

## Typography

- **Display / Headlines:** Space Grotesk / Inter (800 weight, `-0.035em` tracking)
- **UI & PR Reports:** Inter / Geist Sans
- **CLI, SHAs & YAML:** JetBrains Mono (tabular numerals)

## Voice Examples

- CLI: `Lifting 48 commits from upstream… 3 hooks re-applied, 0 brand leaks.`
- PR title: `[Forklift] Sync upstream → v0.1.14`
- Badge states: `Ready to merge` · `Hook Re-applied` · `Owned · Untouched` · `Brand Leaks Fixed`
